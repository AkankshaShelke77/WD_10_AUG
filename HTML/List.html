Virat Kohli
Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer and former all-format captain of the national team,
widely regarded as one of the greatest batters in history. A right-handed top-order batter with 85 international centuries,
he holds the record for the most ODI tons and is the IPL’s leading run-scorer.
Kohli led India to historic Test wins, including its first series victory in Australia, and won multiple ICC trophies such as the 2011 World Cup,
2013 & 2025 Champions Trophies, and 2024 T20 World Cup.
